## Experience goal

The player enters a ruined amusement park as a maintenance technician. Exploration and environmental tasks reveal how extraction damaged the landscape and how water reinjection may slow the collapse.

## Unity prototype

Circular and triangular layouts guide players through platforming, encounters and four reinjection points. Unity systems connect movement, triggers, objectives and environmental changes into one progression flow.

## Testing and iteration

Playtests revealed unclear goals, weak navigation cues and limited feedback. Iteration strengthened landmarks, mission prompts and state changes, producing a clearer route through the restoration sequence.
