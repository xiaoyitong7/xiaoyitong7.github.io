## From research to play

Oyster reefs support coastal ecosystems but are easily overlooked. The project turns restoration research into a playable system that makes ecological relationships easier to understand.

## Design process

Research informed the map, card hierarchy and resource model. Players plant oysters, respond to environmental events and work toward a stable reef while balancing limited resources. Colour, icons and information hierarchy clarify the state of the system at each turn.

## Iteration

Three rounds of playtesting exposed issues in resource balance, event frequency and pacing. Each round informed rule and information changes, resulting in a clearer strategy loop and a more coherent visual system.
