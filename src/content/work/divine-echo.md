## Experience framing

Research into digital divination and ritual shaped a simple experience goal: create a private moment in which a visitor can ask a question and receive an ambiguous response.

## Interaction flow

Visitors approach the altar, bring their palms together and enter a question. Each action produces a visible spatial response, from the projected environment to the particle-based deity, so the ritual remains understandable without written instruction.

## Spatial feedback

Webcam input, text and projection connect the physical altar to the virtual presence. Candlelight, fabric and acrylic stands direct attention while keeping the technology secondary to the visitor's reflective experience.
