## Audience input

Visitors arrange physical letter blocks into a short self-evaluation. A camera reads the word and an AI system classifies its tone as positive or negative.

## Input-to-output mapping

The classification controls two visual response systems around the visitor's live silhouette. Four surrounding screens represent gaze, language, networks and crowds, making the relationship between input and response visible in the space.

## Visitor experience

Positive language creates a warm, breathing outline. Negative language fragments the silhouette before it gathers again. The contrast gives visitors immediate feedback while leaving room to reflect on how judgement changes self-perception.
