## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Yitong Xiao as an Interaction Designer who connects product thinking with expressive interactive media. Success means that a recruiter can identify relevant skills within the homepage, then open a case study and understand the problem, flow, implementation and reflection without relying on inflated claims.

## Positioning

## Product Principles

1. Lead with interaction thinking, then show the medium and technology.
2. Make process legible through problem, flow, prototype, test and reflection.
3. Prefer authentic evidence over decorative claims or simulated results.
4. Keep the interface expressive enough for IME while easy for recruiters to scan.
5. Treat accessibility, responsive behavior and performance as part of design quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful alternative text, readable contrast and intentional reduced-motion behavior. Core content and navigation must remain usable without hover or animation.
